## Priority for later improvements

1. Recover missing historic category evidence through explicit offline preview/candidate workflow: high benefit, no network, category-only and exact-ID/title safeguards. Review excluded records separately.
2. Retain Product Details during future user-initiated Kindle acquisitions: high bibliographic visibility, no additional fetch, observational only. Test more permitted localized/responsive fixtures before claiming broad locale support.
3. Consolidate existing Google Books page count/publication and Open Library work year in collapsed source details: small UI benefit, low risk if edition/work labels remain distinct.
4. Verify Kobo card attributes and HB/FAN raw order bibliographic availability with permitted sanitized fixtures. Only then add observational fields. New HB/FAN DTO fields need a separately reviewed contract, not symmetry work.
5. Edition-specific catalogue enrichment or Kindle hydration recovery requires additional source work/requests and explicit scope. Do not infer verified ISBN/series/ownership from storefront marketing or genre hints.

Provider response facts, observational page facts, catalogue selection, reviewed local metadata and ownership must remain separate. More retained metadata must not silently merge editions or change access.
