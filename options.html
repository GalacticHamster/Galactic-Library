Providers

Import your signed-in libraries and manage remembered accounts. Every saved account stays in your combined Library; selection only chooses which account to update.

Add a library

  1. Choose a provider below and open its website.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to import.
  3. On the Galactic Library panel, enter a recognizable account nickname and confirm the signed-in account.
  4. Start the first Full rescan (Sync library on Epic). Keep the provider tab open until it finishes.
  5. Return here to see the saved account and view imported items in Library.

GL does not sign you in. Importing records what the site reports, including owned items and any borrowed or subscription access it identifies. Acquired contents and book lookups are optional later steps.

Contents

Review included works in your books, graphic novels, collections and omnibuses, then compare reviewed contents. Automated discovery currently supports Kindle. Opening this section does not start a scan.

Contents discovery uses the Kindle account selected for updates in Providers. Saved contents from every remembered account appear below. Open the signed-in Kindle panel to scan, pause or resume; changing tabs here never starts a refresh.

Discover included works on Kindle

Find explicit collection descriptions for eligible imported Kindle books with supported Amazon product pages.

Uses: Your imported eligible Kindle books and your signed-in Kindle library tab.

Saves automatically: The statement and its source. It does not confirm which full issues you own.

Your review: Open the signed-in Kindle library below and use Scan eligible books on its Galactic Library panel. Return to Contents, find the book and choose Inspect contents to check suggestions, series version and language.

Example: "Collects Example Series #1-6" proposes six entries for review. No statement means contents remain unknown.

Find missing book details

Look up books that have no Google Books edition details yet. You can leave this workspace while the lookup runs; keep this library tab open.

How this works

Uses: Book titles, known authors and ISBNs, and your Google Books API key from Settings.

Saves automatically: Metadata from a validated ISBN match or a compatible title and author match. A shorter related title with the same author may add only a labelled genre hint.

Your review: Authorless, ambiguous and conflicting results stay in Review book suggestions. Lookups do not confirm purchases or collected contents. Use Sources and undo on a book to undo a saved selection.

Example: A collection with a shortened Google title may receive a Comics genre hint while its ISBN and collected issues remain unconfirmed.

Pause keeps progress for Resume. Reset progress clears the saved lookup position; it does not undo book details already saved.
