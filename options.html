Add a library: import instructions
  1. Choose a provider below and open its website.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to import.
  3. On the Galactic Library panel, enter a recognizable account nickname and confirm the signed-in account.
  4. Start the first Full rescan (Sync library on Epic). Keep the provider tab open until it finishes.
  5. Return here to see the saved account and view imported items in Library.

GL does not sign you in. Importing records what the site reports, including owned items and any borrowed or subscription access it identifies. Contents discovery and book lookups are optional later steps.

How book lookup works

Uses: Book titles, known authors and ISBNs, and your Google Books API key from Settings.

Saves automatically: Book details when the ISBN, or the title and author, clearly match. A related title with the same author may supply only a labelled genre suggestion.

Your review: Results without an author, or with uncertain or conflicting details, stay in Review book suggestions. Check these before applying them. Lookups do not confirm purchases or included works. Sources and undo restores an earlier saved source when available.

Example: A collection with a shortened Google title may receive a Comics genre hint while its ISBN and collected issues remain unconfirmed.

Pause keeps progress for Resume. Clearing the queue discards the saved lookup position; it does not undo book details already saved.
