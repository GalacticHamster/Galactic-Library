Import your signed-in libraries and manage remembered accounts. Every saved account stays in your combined Library; selection only chooses which account to update.

Add a library: import instructions
  1. Choose a provider below and open its website.
  2. Sign in to the account you want to import.
  3. On the Galactic Library panel, confirm the signed-in account when requested. Store scans need a nickname on first setup; returning single-account imports remember it. For Humble and Fanatical, Connect and sync can use an unnamed default account without a new store scan.
  4. Choose Sync Epic library on Epic, Import Nintendo cards on Nintendo, or Full rescan on other providers. Keep the provider tab open until it finishes.
  5. Return here to see the saved account and view imported items in Library.

GL does not sign you in. Importing records what the site reports, including owned items and any borrowed or subscription access it identifies. Contents discovery and book lookups are optional later steps.

    How book lookup works

    Uses: Book titles, known authors and ISBNs, and your Google Books API key from Settings.

    Saves automatically: Book details when the ISBN, or the title and author, clearly match. A related title with the same author may supply only a labelled genre suggestion.

    Your review: Results without an author, or with uncertain or conflicting details, stay in Review book suggestions. Check these before applying them. Lookups do not confirm purchases or included works. Sources and undo restores an earlier saved source when available.

    Example: A collection with a shortened Google title may receive a Comics genre hint while its ISBN and collected issues remain unconfirmed.

    Pause keeps progress for Resume. Clearing the queue discards the saved lookup position; it does not undo book details already saved.

      Every search word must match saved information for one account. Sort breaks ties between equally relevant matches. Date added uses the provider's reported acquisition/add date, not an inferred purchase or first-import date.

              Performance diagnostics

              Export recent HB/FAN lookups, Options timings and worker activity. These bounded diagnostics are collected automatically; no Amazon page, signed-in Kindle account or contents scan is needed.

              Detailed worker timings are kept in memory and are lost when the Galactic Library service worker restarts. The export may include a tiny session breadcrumb about the previous worker; it is not a retained timing journal. Export soon after reproducing a problem. To show detailed provider tools, enable provider diagnostics under Troubleshooting above and reopen the provider page. Kindle acquisition tracing uses its panel checkbox. For detailed Steam timings, open Steam diagnostics in its GL panel and select Profile the next Quick Update. This does not enable App-ID sampling.
